Biography

Cynthia Nordstrom · Costume Design

A blend of Fashion and Costume Design, Cynthia Nordstrom’s range of design has been seen worldwide from Broadway to the Marvel Universe. Based in the NYC area, she is a designer whose advanced color studies with material and light experimentation, have been an integral part of her process to help tell the story.

Cynthia debuted her first gown in Paris while only a senior in Fashion Design, and then turned her focus back to the stage.

Her edgy dance designs started with Disney stages and tours, and she continued designing and producing costumes worldwide for a wide range of tours and shows on stage, ice and screen. For the past 20 years she has partnered with Universal Studios, Sesame Street, Illuminations, Dreamworks, Sanrio, Nintendo, Pokemon, and Moonbug Animation, to fabricate and deliver the debut of character development from off the screen to Live show reality.
